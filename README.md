# Tithi

Finds the Telugu calendar date (masa + tithi) of your birth, and tells you
which Gregorian date in any target year has the same masa + tithi at sunrise.

## How it works

1. Enter your date and time of birth.
2. The app shows your birth masa and tithi (in Telugu).
3. Enter a target year.
4. The app scans that year for the day where the same masa + tithi are
   present at sunrise, and returns the Gregorian date.

Data source: https://www.prokerala.com/astrology/telugu-panchangam/

## Getting the APK

1. Open the **Actions** tab.
2. Tap the latest **Build APK** run.
3. Download the **app-debug** artifact.
4. Unzip it and install `app-debug.apk` on your Android phone.
